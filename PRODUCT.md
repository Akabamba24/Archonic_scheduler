# Archonic Scheduler Product Context

register: product

## Product Purpose

Archonic Scheduler is a service-booking and dispatch MVP for home-service contractors. It helps customers submit service requests and helps companies configure services, prices, crews, work orders, and confirmation messaging.

## Users

- Customers requesting HVAC, electrical, roofing, or described service help.
- Dispatchers reviewing incoming requests and assigning crews.
- Owners or office managers configuring services, prices, trades, and confirmation settings.

## Tone

Clear, operational, calm, and trustworthy. The interface should feel like a practical tool a contractor could use every day, not a decorative marketing page.

## Product Principles

- Customer booking should feel fast and low-friction.
- Company configuration should be visible but not overwhelming.
- Dispatch actions should be predictable and familiar.
- Demo mode should feel polished enough to show, while clearly separating demo delivery from live SMS/email.

## Anti-References

- Generic AI-purple SaaS styling.
- Overdecorated dashboards.
- Unclear custom controls where standard controls would be faster.
- Visual polish that makes dispatch work harder to scan.
